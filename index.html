About Me

Connecting data to policy

With extensive experience at Statistics Indonesia (BPS), I focus on integrating big data architectures and machine learning into the workflow of official statistics. My expertise lies in designing robust data pipelines, from distributed computing using PySpark to deploying AI-driven analysis models.

As a data science professional with over 13 years of cross-sectoral experience, I operate at the intersection of data analytics, economics, and public policy, with a focus on generating evidence-based insights that support inclusive development, institutional effectiveness, and informed policymaking.

My expertise lies in applying Big Data, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Machine Learning (ML) to complex socioeconomic challenges. I have led the development of scalable analytics systems using Python, R, Apache Spark, and Airflow, with hands-on experience in large-scale data environments including Delta Lake, Apache Iceberg, and Hadoop ecosystems. This technical capacity is complemented by practical experience integrating survey data, administrative records, and digital footprints (e.g., Google Places API) to inform program evaluation and policy analysis.
